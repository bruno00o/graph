## How it works

`createMinimap` draws every node, and the edges up to a limit, in a canvas of its own, with the rectangle of the view on top. It draws in a worker when the browser allows it, at a pace bounded by its own cost, so panning and layouts keep their frame rate. The rectangle follows the view at every frame.

`createLegend` reads the scales of the definition: the categories of `color` with their colors and counts, and a few values of `size`. `mountLegend` renders them as plain HTML styled by the theme tokens. The legend writes no text of its own: the titles come from the example.

Under the pointer, an item puts its nodes in the `match` state. A click switches it off: its nodes go to the `hidden` state, styled with a low opacity.

## Try it

- Drag the rectangle of the minimap, or press elsewhere in it to center the view there.
- Scroll over the minimap to zoom the graph.
- Hover a group in the legend, then click it. From the keyboard, use Tab, then Enter or Space.
- Switch the theme: the minimap and the legend follow it.
