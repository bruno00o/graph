## How it works

The graph starts with `layout: force()` in its definition. The button calls `graph.layout.run(layouts[kind], { fit: true })` with another layout from `@bruno00o/graph/layouts`: the definition keeps its own, and the run applies to every free node.

Grid, rings and radial compute their result at once. Nodes glide to their new place over `transitions.layout`, 400 milliseconds by default, and `fit` frames where they land, while they move.

- **Grid** sorts the nodes by group, then by weight, with an `order` function that receives the data of each node.
- **Rings** puts the node with the most edges in the middle and fills rings outward, by degree.
- **Radial** starts from the node with the most edges and places every other node on the ring of its distance to it, next to the node it was reached from.

## Try it

- Press **Layout** to go from one layout to the next.
- Hold Shift and drag around a few nodes, then press **Arrange the selection**: only these nodes move, around their current center, and the others stay where they are.
- Drag a node, then press **Layout** again: a run moves every free node, the dragged one included.
