title: mountMapGraph and exportMapPng
description: Draw a graph inside a MapLibre map, and export the map and the graph as one PNG.
---

```ts
import { exportMapPng, mountMapGraph } from '@bruno00o/graph/map'

function mountMapGraph<S extends string = string, M extends Mark = Mark>(
  map: Map,
  options: MapGraphOptions<S, M>,
): GraphInstance<S, M>

function exportMapPng<S extends string>(
  graph: GraphInstance<S>,
  options?: MapPngOptions,
): Promise<Blob>
```

`map` is a MapLibre GL JS `Map`, version 6 or later. `mountMapGraph` adds a custom layer to its style and returns a [`GraphInstance`](/docs/api/graph-instance). See [Map](/docs/map) for a walkthrough.

## Errors

- `exportMapPng` throws an `Error` for a graph not mounted by `mountMapGraph`, and a `RangeError` for a `pixelRatio` that is not a positive number or a map smaller than one pixel.
- `exportMapPng` rejects when the hidden map fails to load its style. Tiles that are still missing after `timeout` are left out of the image.

## Behavior

- The graph follows the map's camera at every frame of the map, and draws nothing when the map does not render: an idle map costs nothing.
- `graph.view.set`, `fit`, `zoomBy` and the keyboard move the map with `jumpTo`, one step per frame for animated calls. `update({ view })` is ignored.
- During a node drag or a lasso, the map's drag pan and box zoom are off. Over a node, its double click zoom is off. They come back as they were.
- `destroy` removes the layer and the graph element, and gives back rotation, pitch and the largest pitch of the map. When the style changes, the layer is added again.
- The graph follows the flat and the globe projections, and the change from one to the other. On the globe, elements on the far side are hidden and never picked.
- A layout, `background` and `bundling` in the definition are ignored, and `graph.layout.run` rejects with an `Error`. In development, a layout logs a warning once. The lens and the minimap are not supported. Hulls, groups and the legend work; the `padding` of group containers is in pixels.
