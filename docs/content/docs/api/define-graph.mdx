---
title: defineGraph
description: Build a frozen graph definition from marks, scales, states and options.
---

```ts
function defineGraph<S extends string = never, M extends Mark = never>(
  options: GraphDefinitionOptions<S, M>,
): GraphDefinition<S, M>
```

Returns a frozen definition. `S` is inferred from the keys of `states` and types the state methods of the instance. `M` is inferred from `marks`, the union of their types: the item types and names of the marks type the targets of events and of the state. See [TypeScript](/docs/typescript).

## Layout

`layout` takes a `Layout`, such as the result of [`force()`](/docs/api/force) or [`grid()`](/docs/api/layouts), or `null`. The port is public: any object with this shape works. See [Write your own layout](/docs/layout#write-your-own-layout).

<api-table path="index.ts" name="Layout" />

`start` receives a `LayoutGraph`, whose arrays are indexed by the internal index of each node:

<api-table path="index.ts" name="LayoutGraph" />

A [box](/docs/marks#boxes) is wider than it is tall, so `radius` holds the radius of the circle around it, which keeps layouts that only read `radius` free of overlaps. When the graph has at least one box, `extents` also gives the half width and the half height of every node, two numbers per index: a layout that places nodes in rows or columns can use them to pack boxes closer.

When groups are unfolded, `groups` describes them. Their containers are not nodes of the layout (`alive` is `0` at their index), but their index has a key and an item. A layout that ignores `groups` still works: the graph does not move containers apart for it.

## Bundling

<api-table path="index.ts" name="BundleGraph" />

The bundling reports one polyline per listed edge, in the same order, through `end`:

<api-table path="index.ts" name="BundlePaths" />

<api-table path="index.ts" name="BundleListener" />

<api-table path="index.ts" name="BundleRun" />

`start` returns a `BundleRun`, which the graph stops when nodes move before the paths arrive or when the bundling changes. Two bundlings with the same `name`, `maxEdges` and `options`, compared one by one, are equal for the graph: a new definition with an equal bundling does not compute again, and a new `strength` alone eases the drawn edges to it.

## Routing

`routing` takes an `EdgeRouting`, such as the result of [`orthogonalRouting()`](/docs/api/routing), or `null`. A definition takes `bundling` or `routing`, not both. The port is public: any object with this shape works. See [Edge routing](/docs/routing).

<api-table path="index.ts" name="EdgeRouting" />

`start` receives a `RouteGraph`: what a bundling receives, plus the box of each node and the group that holds it.

<api-table path="index.ts" name="RouteGraph" />

The routing reports one polyline per listed edge through `end`, from the center of the source to the center of the target, as a [bundling](#bundling) does. The graph cuts each route where it leaves the shape of its node, rounds its corners by `radius` and draws it through its points, without resampling. Two routings with the same `name`, `maxEdges` and `options` are equal for the graph, and a new `radius` alone redraws the corners without computing again.

## Background

`background: { grid }` draws a grid under everything, on the canvas, so it follows pan and zoom. The canvas stays transparent: the page still paints `--graph-paper` behind it.

<api-table path="index.ts" name="BackgroundOptions" />

The grid has three levels, `spacing` times a power of 4 apart, chosen from the zoom. The finest level fades in as its lines grow from 4 to 16 pixels apart, and lines of the coarser levels blend into `majorColor`, so zooming never shows a jump. See [Drawing layers](/docs/drawing-layers#background-grid).

## Hulls

`hulls` takes the results of [`hull()`](/docs/api/hull), drawn under edges and nodes in their order. A hull that follows a state must name a declared state, or `selected`, `hovered` or `focused`.

## Errors

- `TypeError` when a mark does not come from `nodes()` or `edges()`, or when `interaction.wheel` is not `'zoom'`, `'pan'` or `'page'`.
- `RangeError` when `view.minZoom` is not positive, `view.maxZoom` is not finite or is smaller than `minZoom`, or `view.bounds` holds a value that is not finite or a rectangle whose minimum exceeds its maximum.
- `RangeError` when `transitions.positions`, `transitions.layout`, `time.fade`, `interaction.zoom.speed` or `interaction.autoPan.speed` is not a finite number of 0 or more.
- `RangeError` when `background.spacing`, `bridges.size` or `interaction.lens.radius` is not a positive number, when `interaction.lens.zoom` is not a finite number of at least 1, or when `bridges.maxEdges` is negative or `NaN`.
- `TypeError` when `background.grid` is not `'lines'` or `'dots'`, when a hull does not come from `hull()`, or when `bundling` or `routing` has no `start` function.
- `TypeError` when both `bundling` and `routing` are given, or when `bridges.shape` is not `'arc'` or `'gap'`.
- `RangeError` with more than 29 states besides `selected`, `hovered` and `focused`.
- `TypeError` when a `layer` is not a non-empty string, or when a state of `support` is not a free state of `states`.
- `RangeError` when a mark beyond the first 128 node marks or the first 128 edge marks names a layer.

## Result

<api-table path="index.ts" name="GraphDefinition" />

Options are resolved: `interaction.zoom` and `interaction.autoPan` are `false` or `{ speed }`, `view` holds `minZoom`, `maxZoom` and `bounds` (`null` without bounds), `transitions.positions` and `transitions.layout` are numbers of milliseconds, `background` is `null` or carries its spacing and colors, and `support` is `null` or names its two states, `null` for one left out.
