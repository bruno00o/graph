## How it works

The definition declares one free state, `near`, with a style for each kind: its nodes get an accent outline and a label, its edges an accent stroke, both raised. The `others` style of each kind fades every element outside the state, as long as it has a member.

On `pointerenter`, a node asks `graph.paths.reach(key, { maxCost: 1 })` for itself, its neighbors and the edges between them, and passes the result to `near` as it is. An edge keeps its two ends and itself. On `pointerleave`, `graph.states.clear()` empties `near`, and the whole graph comes back.

## Try it

- Move the pointer over a node: its neighbors stay, the rest fades.
- Move over an edge: only its two ends stay.
- Leave the element: everything comes back.
