## How it works

`bridges` in `defineGraph` makes an edge jump over the edges it crosses. It needs no `routing`: here every edge is straight. Where a horizontal and a vertical line cross, the horizontal one jumps.

- `shape: 'arc'` draws a half circle over the other edge, `shape: 'gap'` a break in the line.
- `size` is the width of a jump in screen pixels. Zoomed out below 1, jumps shrink with the zoom, and under 2 pixels lines cross plainly.
- The dashed edges set `bridge: false`: they neither jump nor are jumped over.

Crossings are found 150 milliseconds after nodes stop moving, on the main thread. The GPU draws the jumps, so panning and zooming cost no search.

## Try it

- Press **Crossings** to switch between arcs, gaps and plain crossings.
- Press **Size** to change the width of the jumps.
- Drag a node at the end of a line: the jumps follow the new crossings once it stops.
