## How it works

- One `shapes()` mark holds four items, each with its own `geometry`: `rings` for the area, its second ring cutting the hole; `center`, `radius` and `inner` for the ring; `x`, `y`, `width`, `height` and `cornerRadius` for the rectangle; `points` for the line, which is never filled.
- The shape marks come first in `marks`, but shapes always draw under the edges and the nodes, right after the background. Between shape marks, the order of `marks` holds: the backdrop draws first, the plots last.
- The backdrop takes `pick: false`: it only decorates, so clicks and the lasso go through it to the background.
- The plots take `minZoom: 1.2`: they fade in once you zoom into the rectangle, and until then they are neither drawn nor picked.
- A click on a shape selects it, as on a node. On `selectionchange`, the example puts the selected shapes, the nodes of their zone and the edges between those nodes in the `zone` state, whose `others` fades every other node, edge and shape.
- The zones, the plots and the nodes take `color` from the same categorical scale: a zone and the nodes inside it share one color. A colored area takes the color as its outline and mixed to 20 % as its fill, a line as its stroke, and `dash` draws the line dashed. Widths and dashes are in screen pixels.
- The legend counts nodes and shapes in each zone. Its `hidden` state takes `hide: true`, so a zone switched off disappears with its nodes and its plots.
- The nodes take their place from `x` and `y`, in the same world units as the shapes, so no layout runs.
- **Frame** calls `graph.view.fit({ shapes: [key] })`, then `fit()` frames the shapes and the nodes together.

## Try it

- Click a zone in the legend to hide it with its nodes, and again to bring it back.
- Press **Outline** to switch the outlines of the areas between solid, dashed and dotted: only the style changes, the geometries are not read again.
- Zoom in on the edge of the ring: it stays smooth at every zoom.
- Click a zone, then another with Shift: the zone state follows the selection. Click the background, or press Escape, to clear it.
- Shift and drag around the line, or around a plot once zoomed in: the lasso takes the shapes whose every point is inside it, and the nodes inside it.
- Drag a node over a shape: nodes always come first, so a shape never takes a node's click. Dragging a shape moves the view.
