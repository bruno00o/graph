## How it works

Five nodes are joined by several edges per pair, some in each direction. Edges between the same two nodes bend apart in symmetric arcs, and an edge alone on its pair stays straight. Edges from a node to itself draw loops that nest around it.

Each edge takes its own `arrow` and `dash` from its data. The `width` and `size` scales are identity functions, so the values in the definition are pixels.

Each edge also has a label, `label: { text: 'name' }`. It sits halfway along the edge, on the arc when the edge is curved, turned in the direction of the edge and kept upright: above the line, or centered on a plate with `background: true`.

**Start flows** adds `flow` to the edges: dashes move from source to target, at 40 pixels per second, and toward the source on the edges whose arrow points back, with a negative speed. Solid edges flow as dashes, dotted edges move their dots, and arrowheads stay still.

The example listens to `pointerenter` and `pointerleave` to show the hovered edge or node below the graph.

## Try it

- Drag a node: the curves, loops and labels follow it.
- Press **Edge labels** to hide the labels, draw them along the line, or put them on a plate.
- Hover an edge to read its direction. Picking follows the curve, not a straight line.
- Zoom in and out: curves keep their shape.
- Press **Start flows** to move the dashes along each edge.
