## How it works

On `contextmenu`, the example calls `originalEvent.preventDefault()` to keep the menu of the browser away, then fills a plain `div` with buttons. `graph.anchors.set(menu, { node, side: 'right', interactive: true })` pins it next to the node, or at the world point of the click for the background. The menu follows the node when the view moves, and `graph.anchors.remove(menu)` closes it.

The actions use the instance: `graph.selection.set`, `graph.view.fit`, and two free states, `marked` with an outline and `off` with `hide: true`.

## Try it

- Right click a node and pick an action.
- Hide a few nodes, then right click the background and show them again.
- Pan or zoom with the menu open: it stays next to its node.
- Click anywhere to close the menu.
