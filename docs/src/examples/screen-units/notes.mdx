## How it works

Two node marks share the graph:

- **Zones** are nodes sized in world units, from 240 to 300. They grow when you zoom in, like the distances between nodes. A pale `fill` and a `stroke` mixed from a theme token turn them into areas, and their label sits at the `center`.
- **Markers** have `units: 'screen'`: a size of 12 means 12 pixels on screen, whatever the zoom. Zoom in, and the markers spread apart without growing, so a dense zone opens up.

Both marks use the identity size scale, `size: (value) => value`, so the size of a node is the number its channel returns.

The button sets `units` to `'world'` on the markers and passes a new definition to `graph.update`. The markers are then 12 world units wide: tiny when zoomed out, large when zoomed in, as the zones are. The zoom level shows at the bottom left.

Edge widths are always in screen pixels, so the links between markers keep their weight at every zoom.

## Try it

- Zoom in on a zone: the markers stay small and the links between them get longer.
- Switch to world units and zoom out: the markers shrink to dots.
- Zoom out far enough and the zone labels disappear, while their circles stay.
