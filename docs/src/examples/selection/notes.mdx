## How it works

The graph reads two plain arrays of a thousand nodes and two thousand edges. Each node has a position in its `x` and `y` fields, so nothing moves on load.

The definition declares a free state, `kept`, whose `others` style gives every element outside it a low opacity and no label, and turns the lasso on with `interaction: { lasso: true }`.

On `selectionchange`, the example collects the selected nodes, their neighbors from `graph.neighbors(key)` and their edges from `graph.edgesOf(key)`, and puts them into `kept` with `graph.states.set`. Everything else fades while the state has members. An empty selection clears the state.

## Try it

- Click a node: only its neighborhood stays visible.
- Hold Shift and drag from an empty area to draw a lasso. The nodes inside join the selection.
- Press Tab to focus the graph, then use the arrow keys to move the focus and Enter to select.
- Press Escape to clear the selection.
