## How it works

Every edge has a `strength` from 0 to 1: strong inside a group, weak between groups. `minZoom` is a style, a function of the item: edges at 0.8 or above have no limit, and the weaker an edge, the further you must zoom in before it shows, up to about five times the first threshold.

Past its `minZoom`, an edge fades in over a quarter of a zoom doubling. The limits are read once, with the data: zooming only compares the zoom with them on the GPU.

The status counts the edges whose `minZoom` lies below the current zoom, from `viewchange`.

## Try it

- Scroll to zoom in on a group: weaker edges fade in, the strongest first.
- Zoom out again: only the strong edges stay, so the groups read clearly.
- Hover where a hidden edge would be: hidden edges are not picked.
