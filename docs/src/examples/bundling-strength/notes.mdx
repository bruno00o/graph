## How it works

Ten groups sit on a circle, on fixed positions, and 900 edges link them at random. `densityBundling` bundles the edges once nodes stand still, in a Web Worker.

`strength` is the share of the bundled shape that is drawn: 0 draws straight lines, 1 the bundles as computed. The slider builds a new definition with `densityBundling({ strength })` at each move. Its other options are the same, so the graph keeps the bundles it has and eases the edges to the new strength, without computing again.

The status counts the computations from `bundling.running` in the store: it stays at one while the slider moves.

## Try it

- Drag the slider from 1 to 0 and back.
- Drag a node: its edges follow, then bundle again once it stops, and the count goes up.
