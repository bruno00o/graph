## How it works

On `click`, a node reads its neighbors with `graph.paths.reach(key, { maxCost: 1 })`, and `graph.view.fit({ ...around, padding, duration })` glides to the box around them: the result of `reach` has the `{ nodes, edges }` shape that `fit` takes. A click on the background fits the whole graph again with `graph.view.fit({ duration })`.

Right after mounting, the example zooms the same way on a node it selected, so the page opens close up. The view follows that node and its neighbors while the first layout settles them. `view.maxZoom` keeps a lone node from filling the screen.

## Try it

- Click a node: the view glides to it and its neighbors.
- Click another node in the frame to move from one neighborhood to the next.
- Click the background to see the whole graph.
